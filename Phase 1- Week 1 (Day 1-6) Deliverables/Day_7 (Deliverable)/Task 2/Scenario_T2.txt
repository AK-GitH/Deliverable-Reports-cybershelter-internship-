Task 2 - Permissions + ACL Scenario

A folder /srv/finance holds the company's finance reports.

1. ak (owner) needs full access: read, write and open the folder.
2. The "team" group (includes member-1) may only read and open the folder, not change anything.
3. Everyone else must have no access at all.
4. An external user "auditor" (not in the team group) must be able to read and write in the folder to add audit notes.
